What does this mean? Forensic Guide
C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) is an open industry standard that embeds cryptographic assertions directly inside images and documents. These "Content Credentials" record the history and origin of a file (e.g., the camera model, editing software, or generative AI model used). While meant for accountability, they leak sensitive workflow, software, and device history. TraceClean strips these JUMBF container boxes and signature manifests.
Image and document containers (like JPEG, WebP, PDF, or DOCX) store structural properties detailing device IDs, editing prompts, geolocation coords, author names, and timestamps. Generative AI tools often inject tags (like Software: Midjourney or DigitalSourceType: TrainedAlgorithmicRepresentation) into XMP and EXIF structures. TraceClean targets and deletes these tags while leaving the main visual or structural data intact.
Invisible steganography exploits hidden Unicode characters (such as zero-width spaces U+200B, tag characters U+E0020, and variations selectors) to silently encode tracking payloads and corporate watermarks inside plain text. The text looks completely normal to the human eye, but copy-pasting it retains these binary carrier signatures. TraceClean detects and normalizes these characters to standard Unicode representations.
Stylometry is the statistical analysis of literary style. Generative AI models write with predictable linguistic patterns, word distributions, and structural repetitions. Our engine evaluates these statistical markers to assign a likelihood score of machine authorship. Unlike physical watermarks, stylometric patterns cannot be stripped automatically without rewriting the text.
